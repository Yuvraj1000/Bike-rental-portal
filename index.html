WHY RIDE HUB

Introduction & Idea Overview

Ride Hub is a simple Bike Rental Portal that gives riders an easy, on-demand way to book and return bikes across campus and the city.

THE PROBLEM

  • Traditional bike rentals mean queues, paperwork & delays
  • Riders need a fast, reliable way to find and book bikes
  • No single platform to track bikes, stations & ride history

OUR IDEA

  • A centralized, digital bike rental platform
  • Simple, rider-friendly booking & return flow
  • Real-time visibility into bike & station availability
  • Promotes affordable, eco-friendly commuting
